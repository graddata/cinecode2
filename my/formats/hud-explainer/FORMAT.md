## The language (kept, and adapted to the new subject)

Each line is a way of making the film, not a value to paste: say on the new card how it meets the new idea. No line names an object, word or image of the source film: those are its content, never the format.

- **Look and medium:** motion design as a technical HUD drawing: a dark board seen in shallow perspective (Canvas 2D VIEW), hairline floor grid fading into the far air, outlined tiles drawn on in drafting order, corner ticks, mono labels on every part; screen space HUD on top (corner brackets, a header line, an event log panel, a big state counter).
- **Palette logic:** near black green tinted ground, pale cool ink for lines and text, a dim grey green for secondary lines; colour means state only: one 'ok' colour, one 'failure' colour, one 'in progress' colour used briefly; glow only on things that are lit by their state; one bright stretch at the payoff and a full accent band at the end.
- **Type style:** one monospaced face for every role (IBM Plex Mono or a sibling), lower case technical labels, captions typed on character by character in a boxed note at the bottom, the title typed with a block cursor.
- **Camera and form:** a screen space terminal opening, then one continuous perspective camera over the board (crane down, truck, a jolt on the failure, push in, pull back) with hard cuts that are invisible, and a dissolve into the title band.
- **Rhythm:** 6 beats in 30 s: 4 s hook, 8 s build, 2 s flow, 6 s failure, 7 s recovery, 3 s title; cuts on 2 s bars at 120 BPM, one continuous camera across the middle
- **Hook:** a terminal command types itself on a near black screen from frame 0, a grid rises under it, Enter sends it
- **Ending:** a full width accent band opens and the thesis line types onto it, the state counter returns under it
- **Sound language:** synthesized ambient on a 120 BPM grid: soft pads and a low drone, a quiet kick pulse, hats whose density mirrors the system's state, dry ticks and taps for every state change, one low dissonant hit for the failure, a short pluck arpeggio as the 'healthy' motif; sparse, one shared room.
- **Atmosphere:** light comes only from the elements themselves (state glow, a brief full frame tint on failure and on recovery); depth from the perspective grid fading to haze and from near, middle and far rows of the diagram.
